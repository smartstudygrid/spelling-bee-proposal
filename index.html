Fairness & Standardization

BUILT-IN CREDIBILITY

Standardized rules protect integrity and ensure transparent outcomes defensible to parents.

Pronouncer Protocol

  • Sequence: Word definition → Sentence context → Word repeat.
  • Both US & UK pronunciations given where variations exist.
  • Designated speller speaks for the team.
  • Strictly zero hints or artificial emphasis.

Judges Protocol

  • Both US & UK spelling variants accepted equally.
  • Capitalization not required unless explicitly specified.
  • All rulings are immediate and final.
  • Dual-adjudicator verification on stage.

The 50-Second Rule

Timed
0–25 Seconds: Ask re-pronunciation, definition, or discuss with teammates.
At 25 Seconds: Active spelling must officially commence.
Up to 50 Seconds: Valid if started before 50s; retracing allowed ("Can I start over?").
